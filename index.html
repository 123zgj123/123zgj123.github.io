Guijia Zhang

Agent security researcher working on safer, more inspectable tool-using systems.

I study the security and safety of agents that take actions. The through-line is to turn real system failures into measurable problems—from request-conditioned safety auditing to evidence-grounded authorization and runtime regulation.

Research Profile

I have research experience across the full research loop: problem formulation, benchmark construction, risk modeling, system validation, and scientific writing. I collaborate with researchers at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, KAUST, HKUST (Guangzhou), and Tsinghua University.

Research interests: agent security and safety · trustworthy AI · LLM reasoning · agent systems · code LLM security
